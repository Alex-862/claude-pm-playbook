# Discovery Synthesis

## Purpose

This skill helps synthesise ambiguous discovery context into:
- themes
- tensions
- opportunity spaces
- assumptions
- missing information
- recommended areas for deeper exploration

This is an exploratory workflow and should not immediately jump to solution definition or PRD generation.

---

## Inputs

Typical inputs may include:
- customer insights
- analytics
- support themes
- strategic goals
- operational pain points
- market observations
- business constraints

---

## Behaviour

When using this skill:

1. Summarise the discovery context
2. Identify:
   - recurring themes
   - customer pain points
   - behavioural patterns
   - business tensions
3. Highlight:
   - missing information
   - assumptions
   - conflicting signals
4. Suggest:
   - opportunity spaces
   - areas for further research
   - promising directions for deeper exploration

---

## Important Constraints

- Do not jump directly to implementation
- Do not draft a PRD unless explicitly requested
- Avoid pretending certainty where evidence is weak
- Clearly separate:
  - evidence
  - assumptions
  - hypotheses

---

## Outputs

Typical outputs may include:
- discovery-synthesis.md
- opportunity-options.md
- assumptions.md
- research-gaps.md

## Example Prompt

Use the discovery-synthesis skill against the following folders:

- business-context/
- discovery/

Write the output to:

- outputs/discovery-synthesis.md

Do not draft a PRD.
Do not propose implementation details yet.
Focus on themes, assumptions, gaps, tensions, and opportunity areas.
