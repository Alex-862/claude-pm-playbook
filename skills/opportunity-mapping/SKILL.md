# Opportunity Mapping

## Purpose

This skill helps transform broad discovery insights into clearer opportunity spaces.

It is intended to support:
- prioritisation
- strategic discussion
- initiative selection
- hypothesis shaping

---

## Inputs

Typical inputs:
- discovery synthesis
- customer insights
- analytics
- strategic goals
- market context

---

## Behaviour

When using this skill:

1. Identify candidate opportunity spaces
2. For each opportunity:
   - define the customer problem
   - explain why it matters
   - identify potential strategic value
   - assess differentiation potential
   - identify risks and constraints
3. Compare opportunities across:
   - customer value
   - strategic fit
   - feasibility
   - confidence
   - evidence quality

---

## Important Constraints

- Do not assume every opportunity should proceed
- Highlight weak evidence or uncertainty
- Avoid over-optimistic assumptions
- Keep opportunities at initiative level, not implementation detail

---

## Outputs

Typical outputs:
- opportunity-options.md
- opportunity-scorecard.md
- selected-direction.md

## Example Prompt

Use the opportunity-mapping skill against:

- outputs/discovery-synthesis.md
- business-context/
- discovery/

Write the output to:

- outputs/opportunity-options.md

Compare opportunities across:
- customer value
- strategic fit
- differentiation
- feasibility
- risk
- evidence quality

Do not select a final direction unless explicitly asked.
