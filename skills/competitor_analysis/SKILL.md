# Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

This skill supports comparative analysis against competitors, challengers and adjacent products.

The goal is not to copy competitors directly, but to:
- understand market patterns
- identify convergence
- identify gaps
- identify differentiation opportunities

---

## Behaviour

When using this skill:

1. Identify relevant competitors or comparable experiences
2. Summarise:
   - positioning
   - customer value proposition
   - key UX patterns
   - notable strengths or weaknesses
3. Compare against the current product or opportunity area
4. Highlight:
   - common industry patterns
   - unmet needs
   - possible differentiation opportunities

---

## Important Constraints

- Avoid simplistic feature comparison
- Focus on customer outcomes and strategic positioning
- Do not assume competitor success automatically translates
- Clearly separate:
  - observed behaviour
  - inferred strategy
  - speculation

---

## Outputs

Typical outputs:
- competitor-analysis.md
- market-patterns.md
- differentiation-opportunities.md
